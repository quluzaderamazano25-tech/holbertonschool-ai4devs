## Problem Statement
Students and early-career professionals often feel overwhelmed by the complexity of choosing a career path and don't know which skills to prioritize first.

## MVP Features
- Skill assessment quiz to determine user interests.
- AI-generated career roadmap with specific learning resources.
- Progress tracker to visualize skill acquisition.
- Weekly AI-driven motivation and job market insights.
